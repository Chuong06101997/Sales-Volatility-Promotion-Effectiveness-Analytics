# Risk Register

**Project:** Sales Volatility, Promotion Effectiveness & Forecasting Benchmark Review
**Status:** Canonical governance document. Third of three governance documents, alongside the Decision Log and Assumption Register.
**Purpose:** Documents what could go wrong in the future that would reduce the reliability, validity, applicability, or future usefulness of this project's conclusions. The Decision Log records why choices were made; the Assumption Register records what those choices depend on; this register records what could change and undermine them.

## Governance Perspective

The Decision Log records the analytical choices made during this project and why each was chosen over its alternatives. The Assumption Register records what those choices depend on — the conditions that must hold for the analysis to remain valid. This Risk Register records what could change in the future that would threaten those same conditions.

Together, the three documents provide complete analytical governance: a reader can trace any conclusion in the README, Executive Summary, or Full Report back to the decision that produced it (Decision Log), the assumption that decision relies on (Assumption Register), and the risk that assumption could someday fail (Risk Register). No conclusion in this project rests on an unexamined choice, an unstated assumption, or an unacknowledged future risk.
